## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-performance clinical workspace engineered for mobile triage, night-shift rounding, and critical alert routing. The visual ethos combines deep obsidian liquidity with luminous optical conduits. It treats the interface as a physical pane of dark, polarized volcanic glass illuminated by crisp, sub-surface phosphors.

The aesthetic fuses Liquid Glassmorphism with mission-critical ergonomic utility:
- **Atmosphere**: Oceanic midnight depths with zero light pollution, ensuring zero ocular fatigue during dark ward operations.
- **Surface Texture**: Dense, light-refracting dark vitreous sheets stacked over abyssal indigo fields.
- **Luminance**: High-contrast, optical-grade photon emission across text and semantic indicators, ensuring rapid legibility under emergency conditions.
- **Affect**: Serene, hyper-precise, authoritative, and instantaneous.

## Colors

The palette balances low-emissivity backdrops with high-potency photon accents. Every element meets strict contrast thresholds against ambient dark hospital environments while preventing retinal burn-in.

### Canvas & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#070b14`)**: Abyssal black-navy substrate. Absorbs ambient light and grounds floating glass sheets.
- **Canvas Elevated (`#0b1120`)**: Structural grounding for persistent app bars and docked navigation.
- **Glass Fill (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.75)`)**: Sub-surface midnight slate matrix. Provides optical thickness and isolates foreground clinical data from contextual ambient movement.
- **Glass Highlights (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`)**: Directional overhead refraction lines.

### Primary Accents & Conduits
- **Primary Cyan (`#38bdf8`)**: Active conduits, focused touch targets, and critical path telemetry.
- **Azure Flow (`#0ea5e9`)**: Dynamic states, secondary active elements, and progress metrics.
- **Deep Cobalt (`#2563eb`)**: Structural selection layers, pressed fills, and passive telemetry.

### Semantic Triage Accents
- **Emergency / Critical (`#ef4444`)**: Immediate code red, cardiac arrest, patient distress calls. Associated glow: `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.35)`.
- **Warning / Pending (`#f59e0b`)**: Fall alerts, drip rate warnings, unacknowledged routine notices. Associated glow: `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.3)`.
- **Stable / Verified (`#10b981`)**: Normal telemetry, administered medication confirmations, acknowledged calls. Associated glow: `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`.

### Typography Colors
- **Primary Text (`#f8fafc`)**: Crystalline white with an optical anti-halation tint.
- **Muted Text / Metadata (`#94a3b8`)**: Cool atmospheric slate for secondary timestamps, unit tags, and field keys.
- **Subtle Trace (`#64748b`)**: Inactive iconography, track dividers, and inactive toggles.

## Typography

The pairing between the technical, angular cadence of Space Grotesk and the human-centered clarity of Plus Jakarta Sans creates a hierarchy optimized for rapid clinical cognition.

- **Display & Bedside Metrics (`Space Grotesk`)**: Room identifiers, vital sign data streams, and alert status titles leverage technical, geometric glyph forms. Numerals possess distinct optical envelopes to prevent misinterpretation of dosages or room numbers.
- **Narrative & Observational Text (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Patient symptoms, shift notes, and medication administration records use high-legibility geometric forms with generous x-heights, ensuring clarity at low screen brightness levels.
- **Data Densities**: Labels at `label-sm` use all-caps with generous letter-spacing (`0.04em`) to designate status tags, triage classifications, and milligram dosages cleanly without visual crowding.

## Layout & Spacing

A strictly regimented 4px/8px incremental spatial rhythm governs this system, built for one-handed thumb interaction during clinical movement.

### Structural Grid & Canvases
- **Mobile Handheld (360px - 430px)**: Single-column liquid layout with `margin-mobile` (16px) dynamic screen insets. Component stacks utilize `space-md` gaps.
- **Tablet / Bedside Workstation (768px - 1024px)**: 6 to 8-column adaptive grid with `gutter` (16px) separation and `margin` (20px) outer edge pads. Allows split-view routing: call list on the left pane, vital signs and intervention workflows on the right.
- **Desktop Ward Console (1200px+)**: 12-column layout pinned to a max-width of 1440px to retain situational awareness without requiring broad ocular scanning.

### Ergonomic Safe Margins
- **Bottom Reach Zone**: Primary operational switches, acknowledge buttons, and push-to-talk triggers must reside within an 88px clearance zone above the native home indicator.
- **Touch Target Integrity**: No interactive surface spans less than 48px in height or width, surrounded by an isolation boundary of `space-xs`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use solid shadows. Instead, it relies on refractive liquid optics: stacked dark-translucent volumes, multi-layered backdrop blurs, and localized edge illumination.

### The Refractive Tier Architecture

1. **Substrate (Z: 0)**: Solid `#070b14` canvas. Absorbs base interactions.
2. **Glass Level 1 - Base Containers (Z: 1)**:
   - Background: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.75)`
   - Backdrop Filter: `blur(24px) saturate(180%)`
   - Border: 1px solid with top-to-bottom linear gradient: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` down to `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.04)`
   - Shadow: `0 8px 32px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`
3. **Glass Level 2 - Floating Cards & Active Beds (Z: 2)**:
   - Background: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.85)`
   - Backdrop Filter: `blur(32px) saturate(200%)`
   - Border: 1px solid `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.22)`
   - Shadow: `0 12px 40px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`, complemented by an inner hairline glow: `inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`
4. **Glass Level 3 - Overlays, Emergency Modals & Dynamic Alerts (Z: 3)**:
   - Background: `rgba(11, 17, 32, 0.92)`
   - Backdrop Filter: `blur(40px) saturate(210%)`
   - Border: 1px solid `rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.45)`
   - Shadow: `0 24px 64px 0 rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`

### Semantic Phosphor Auroras
When critical events trigger, cards project a soft, atmospheric colored cast rather than a hard boundary:
- **Code Red / Urgent Distress**: Ambient drop field of `0 0 35px -5px rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.35)` paired with a directional edge highlight of `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.5)`.
- **System Stable / Acknowledged**: Subtle edge pulse of `0 0 25px -8px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces organic curvature tempered by clinical structure. Rounding tokens maintain continuity between small interactive chips and large triage surface sheets.

- **Base Radii (`0.5rem` / 8px)**: Segmented switch elements, internal sub-metrics, status chips, and input text fields.
- **Card Radii (`1rem` / 16px)**: Patient queue cards, call notification pods, and diagnostic readouts.
- **Overlay & Modal Radii (`1.5rem` / 24px)**: Emergency alert sheets, sliding telemetry drawers, and quick-response action docks.
- **Concentric Offset Rule**: Nested containers dynamically adjust their outer curvature. An inner component encased in a 16px corner pocket uses an 8px radius to preserve uniform fluid spacing.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary / Urgent Intervention**: Background spans a rich directional gradient (`#0ea5e9` to `#2563eb`). Surface exhibits an inner glass bevel (`inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`), crisp white text (`#f8fafc`), and a localized aura shadow (`0 4px 20px rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary Glass**: Liquid background (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.75)`), perimeter stroke (`rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.2)`), text color (`#38bdf8`). Active tap induces a high-refraction sheen (`rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.15)`).
- **Critical Code Button**: Crimson fire gradient (`#ef4444` to `#b91c1c`), high-frequency pulsing halo, bold display typography.

### Cards (Patient Call & Vital Pods)
- Encapsulated within Glass Level 2 specifications.
- Divided into three functional zones: 
  1. Room header with high-contrast Space Grotesk identifiers and semantic status beacon.
  2. Elastic body containing vital sparklines and patient alert details.
  3. Action strip with direct push-to-talk, acknowledge, and triage reassignment triggers.
- Alerting states introduce an active breathing pulse across the perimeter border.

### Status Badges & Chips
- Compact pill-shaped enclosures.
- Background: Translucent wash of the parent semantic value at 15% opacity (e.g., `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.15)` for urgent alerts).
- Border: 1px hairline matching the semantic hue at 40% opacity.
- Indicator: Embedded 6px circular phosphor dot projecting an atmospheric glow, accompanied by uppercase `label-sm` text.

### Form Inputs & Telemetry Adjusters
- Height: 52px to ensure reliable finger access during transit.
- Background: `rgba(7, 11, 20, 0.6)`.
- Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`. Shifts to `#38bdf8` with an external field glow (`0 0 12px rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.25)`) on focus.
- Typography: Active values enter in `#f8fafc`; placeholders remain at `#64748b`.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- **Track**: Inset obsidian channel with inner stroke shadowing (`inset 0 2px 4px rgba(0,0,0,0.5)`).
- **Thumb / Active State**: Frosted cyan glass prism (`rgba(56, 189, 248, 0.2)`) bounded by a bright cyan border (`#38bdf8`), sliding over dark obsidian tracks.

### NurseCall Push-to-Talk HUD (Domain Specific)
- Persistent floating glass capsule pinned to the ergonomic thumb region.
- Contains an active audio waveform visualization glowing in `#38bdf8`, room connection breadcrumbs, and an instantaneous mechanical disconnect switch bordered in `#ef4444`.
